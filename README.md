## 🔬 About & Research Overview
Dr. Ayan Mukherjee's research focuses on fundamental and applied electrochemistry, with particular emphasis on electrode kinetics, electrocatalytic interfaces, materials synthesis, and advanced energy storage systems. His group works at the intersection of physical chemistry, interfacial phenomena, and material characterization to address challenges in sustainable energy technologies.

### Key Research Themes
* **Electrochemical Energy Storage:** Redox mechanisms, solid-electrolyte interfaces, and battery/supercapacitor design.
* **Electrocatalysis & Kinetics:** Cyclic voltammetry analysis, reaction kinetics, and novel functional materials for catalytic conversion.
* **Interfacial Electrochemistry:** Surface modification, charge transfer transport dynamics, and impedance spectroscopy ($EIS$).
