## 🔬 Overview

This repository presents an academic overview of the research activities of:

- **Dr. Ayan Mukherjee**
- **Prof. Partha Mitra**
- **Roupya Gopal Ghosh**

with particular emphasis on:

- Electrochemistry
- Functional thin films
- Electrodeposition
- Transition-metal-oxide electrodes
- Supercapacitors
- Pseudocapacitive energy storage
- Electrochemical characterization
- Nanostructured materials
- Binder-free electrode architectures

Recent collaborative research has focused on **NiCo₂O₄, Co₃O₄ and Zn-doped Co₃O₄ thin-film electrodes** for electrochemical energy-storage applications.

Dr. Ayan Mukherjee is associated with research in **thin films, nanostructured materials, functional oxide materials and electrochemical energy-storage systems**.

### Research Interests

- Thin-film synthesis
- Electrodeposition
- Transition-metal oxides
- Nanostructured materials
- Supercapacitors
- Electrochemical energy storage
- Electrochemical characterization
- Functional semiconductor materials
- Electrical and optical properties of thin films

Recent research associated with Dr. Mukherjee includes electrodeposited **NiCo₂O₄ and Co₃O₄-based thin-film electrodes** for supercapacitor applications.
